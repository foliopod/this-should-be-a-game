Talking with Marn ([marns.itch.io](https://marns.itch.io/), [corpserevivers](https://bsky.app/profile/corpserevivers.bsky.social) on Bluesky) about how to turn 2017's _Happy Death Day_ into a TTRPG. Check out [the playtest edition of MetaFiction](https://marns.itch.io/metafiction) and find Batman: Bump in the Night wherever you get your podcasts.

Join the [Haunted Table Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/HauntedTableGames) at $3 or above to playtest The House Edge, a roguelike time loop Triangle Agency/FIST: Ultra cross-over TTRPG.
