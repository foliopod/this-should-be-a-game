Responding to listener submissions about how to make 2017's _Happy Death Day_ into a TTRPG.

[The inaugural This Should Be A Game! Jam](https://itch.io/jam/this-should-be-a-game-jam-01) is open for submissions from August 3rd to September 3rd– make a game related to any of the movies we've covered so far on the show, or the conversations we've had about them!

Plus: the mechanical mailbag is open for the next double feature, as voted on by the members of our Patreon: **John Carpenter's Halloweenless Halloween**! [Submit your ideas](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd0CQrOA4tqSAbCEduiaiyj_y60CU3s4k_6_Q52MPmRMILXwQ/viewform) for TTRPGs and TTRPG material inspired by *In the Mouth of Madness* or *The Thing* and we just might discuss them on the show. The mailbag closes at 11:59 PM EST on August 27th.

Join the [Haunted Table Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/HauntedTableGames) at $3 or above to playtest The House Edge, a roguelike time loop Triangle Agency/FIST: Ultra cross-over TTRPG.

**Games referenced:** [Firebands](https://lumpley.itch.io/firebrands), [It's Been A Long Long Time](https://metagame.itch.io/its-been-a-long-long-time), [It's Been A Long Time Coming](https://metagame.itch.io/its-been-a-long-time-coming), [Yazeba's Bed and Breakfast](https://possumcreekgames.itch.io/yazebas-bed-breakfast), [Triangle Agency](https://hauntedtable.itch.io/triangle-agency), [Citizen Sleeper](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1578650/Citizen_Sleeper/), [Time After Time](https://headofthegoat.itch.io/time-after-time)
