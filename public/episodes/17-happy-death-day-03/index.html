Happy Death Day - 03 - Mechanical Mailbag

2026-08-03 📼 Happy Death Day 👥 Mechanical Mailbag None

Responding to listener submissions about how to make 2017’s Happy Death Day into a TTRPG.

The inaugural This Should Be A Game! Jam is open for submissions from August 3rd to September 3rd– make a game related to any of the movies we’ve covered so far on the show, or the conversations we’ve had about them!

Plus: the mechanical mailbag is open for the next double feature, as voted on by the members of our Patreon: John Carpenter’s Halloweenless Halloween! Submit your ideas for TTRPGs and TTRPG material inspired by In the Mouth of Madness or The Thing and we just might discuss them on the show. The mailbag closes at 11:59 PM EST on August 27th.

Join the Haunted Table Patreon at $3 or above to playtest The House Edge, a roguelike time loop Triangle Agency/FIST: Ultra cross-over TTRPG.

Games referenced: Firebands, It’s Been A Long Long Time, It’s Been A Long Time Coming, Yazeba’s Bed and Breakfast, Triangle Agency, Citizen Sleeper, Time After Time
